# Повторный вызов своего исполнителя (draft, 18.11.2026)

DRAFT / NOT MERGED / NOT DEPLOYED.

Users can pick a performer from real previous completed orders in their fleet. The app passes preferredPerformerId to the order API; backend verifies a prior completed job from the same client and the appropriate role. A user cannot target an arbitrary known account ID.

When dispatch opens, an approved, on-line, genuinely free, in-radius and service-compatible previously hired performer gets one first invitation. Progressive SOS road ETA restrictions remain in force if activated. The worker must press Accept; customers cannot auto-assign a person. If busy, unavailable, too far, off shift, blocked, or declining/timeout, the existing priority or auction process continues. No special price or guaranteed arrival; customer sees this before sending.

Any favorite without completed work remains a marker, not a direct request. An existing normal repeat-call path remains available. No amendments to accepted design or money processing.

Verification gates: separate testing across cash and real online holds, concurrent invitations and two devices, selected offline/busy, customer blacklisting, denied user IDs, drive time route gate, Android, full CI, no production rollout without acceptance.
