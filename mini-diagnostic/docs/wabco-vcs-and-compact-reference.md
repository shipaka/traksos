# WABCO VCS I, Compact Tester II and legacy part-number research
26.11.2026. VCS I: manufacturer's Blinkcode Vario Compact, https://www.etsp.ru/documents/blink_codes_vcs_.pdf, printed pages 6–7, PDF index 3 visually checked. 39 exact documented component/fault combinations: 16 sensors, 16 modulator/retarder, 7 system. Third group is component occurrence count, not a third part of the error identity; manual lookup uses only two groups. No flash triggering or clear/parameterization code. Not compatible with Vario C or VCS II by assumption.
Compact Tester II: official WABCO https://www.wabco-customercentre.com/catalog/docs/8150101073.pdf, printed pp8,10,11 (indices 5,7,8) visually checked. 20 path and 19 fault-type symbols are independent fields on the tester, including non-trailer fields; NOT 380 verified combinations. Third symbol 1–9 indicates count, 9 means nine or more. Current vs historical depends on where the external tester is in its display sequence, not this code. No inference from 'old' or system ID. Separate display namespace, not raw DTC.
Seven published part-number ranges and associated data-link types added as search leads, not individual validated part numbers, exact identity or permission to transmit. Vario C and VCS I: JED 677; listed VCS II ranges KWP2000; TCS I/II SAE J1587 (future market until power and interface verified). TCS entries are not automatically compatible with 24 V device. No guessing part number from CAN ID. Manual selection remains unverified; no active profile.
