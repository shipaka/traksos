The individual scenes are composed into desktop headings with an alpha
gradient on the left, so the title/description remain live HTML and no
opaque right-hand rectangle or duplicated lorry appears. A scene belongs
to ONE approved actual role; do not create phantom workspaces for TrakBot,
generic partners, or "service and development".

not the high-resolution canonical masters required for future mobile/Android
exports. The separate 1600×240 source panoramas are prepared but need
promotion to this repository with provenance and real screenshot approval
before replacing the sheet. Simple upscaling must not be called HQ.

scenes are approved. The distinct approved courier desk layout is preserved;
the courier scene is reserved rather than silently changing its header.
